<!--more-->

MedMemoryBench targets the evaluation of memory in multi-turn medical dialogue, where systems must retain and use clinically relevant information across long interactions. The benchmark and accompanying resources cover roughly 2,000 sessions, 16,000 turns, and 1,939 questions.

The work provides a benchmark for studying reliable memory behavior in medical language-model applications.
