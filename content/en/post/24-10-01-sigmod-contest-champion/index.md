At the SIGMOD 2024 Programming Contest, two joint teams formed by Zhejiang University and Southern University of Science and Technology reached the global finals under the joint guidance of Huan Li and Bo Tang. Team Alaya won the championship.

Alaya included Bowen Zeng from the SuDIS team, while the other finalist team included students from Zhejiang University. The result marked the team's first participation in, and championship at, the SIGMOD programming contest.
