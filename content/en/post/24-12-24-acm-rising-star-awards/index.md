The ACM SIGMOD China Rising Star Award recognizes promising young researchers in data management. Huan Li was selected as one of the 2024 recipients and was recommended to the ACM China Rising Star Award.

The subsequent ACM China announcement listed Huan Li among the Honorable Mention recipients. The two recognitions reflect contributions to data-centric AI, efficient inference, and data systems.
