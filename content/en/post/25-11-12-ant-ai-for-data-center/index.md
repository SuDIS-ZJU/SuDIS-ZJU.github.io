Zhejiang University and Ant Group established a joint research center focused on artificial intelligence for data. SuDIS participates in the collaboration, which connects data management, model efficiency, and practical data-intensive applications.

The public report describes the center as a collaborative platform. It does not assign a single principal investigator or claim a specific project outcome.
